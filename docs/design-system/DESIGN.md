## Brand & Style
This design system crafts an intentional digital sanctuary centered around holistic emotional health, guided mindfulness, and contemplative restoration. Built for individuals seeking balance, self-reflection, and inner equilibrium, the experience moves away from sterile clinical software or gamified hyper-stimulation toward organic warmth, clarity, and safety.

The visual style blends modern organic minimalism with soft tactile nuances. Generous negative space gives thoughts room to breathe, while soft warm off-white canvases remove eye strain. Visual weight is communicated through warm tonal shifts and gentle atmospheric depth rather than hard divisions or jarring signals. Interaction patterns invite deliberate, centered pacing.

**Note on palette intent**: unlike a typical "calm app" that leans on cool sage/lavender as the dominant tone, Moodbeat deliberately leads with **warm terracota**. The brand's calming promise comes from tonal warmth, generous whitespace and soft shapes — not from a cold, muted palette. Sage and lavender remain present as grounding secondary/tertiary accents, not as the dominant register.

## Colors
The palette is grounded in natural transitions of light, stone, foliage, and twilight:

- **Primary (`#D9653B`)** — dominant color of the UI: Sunset Terracotta provides life, warmth, and vitality. Used for primary calls to action, active engagement states, heart rate/energy check-ins, focal milestones, and the majority of high-emphasis surfaces (buttons, active nav, selected chips, highlighted cards).
- **Secondary (`#4E7058`)**: Deep Moss Sage acts as an anchor of calm, used sparingly. Applied to grounded states, progress loops, nature meditations, tranquil badges, and equilibrium markers.
- **Tertiary (`#6D5B8A`)**: Twilight Lavender conveys depth, dusk, and contemplation, used sparingly. Reserved for evening journaling prompts, dream logs, reflective tags, and quiet breathwork prompts.
- **Neutral (`#766A65`)**: Warm Earth Stone shapes balanced typography, subtle borders, and harmonious structure.

Background surfaces rely on warm ivory and soft alabaster layers (`#FDFBF7`, `#F7F3EC`, `#EFE9DF`), eliminating cold stark whites. Semantic tokens adapt these pigments across varying alpha channels to preserve a quiet, soft-contrast canvas.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm sets an open, grounded mood. `Epilogue` delivers warmth and human craftsmanship through its architectural, softly sculptural character—ideal for introspective headlines, breathing milestones, and daily wisdom. `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides a clean, open, and comforting sans-serif partner with friendly terminals and high legibility across long-form journaling, prompts, and interface navigation.

All heading sizes taper smoothly on viewport adjustments to maintain proportional grace without feeling assertive or crowded. Body leading is deliberately loosened to facilitate serene reading and cognitive ease.

## Layout & Spacing
A 12-column responsive fluid grid provides flexible composition while maintaining comfortable whitespace. Layout rules follow an 8pt spatial baseline, shifting down to 4pt only for compact micro-labels.

- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column structure with `margin-mobile` (20px) and `gutter-sm` (16px). Single-column flow is favored to minimize distraction and cognitive clutter.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px)**: 8-column structure with `margin-tablet` (32px) and `gutter-md` (24px). Dual-panel splits accommodate companion trackers, reflection logs, or audio meditation players.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column structure with `margin-desktop` (56px) and `gutter-lg` (32px), constrained to a maximum content width of 1200px (`max-content-width`) to preserve balanced sightlines.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is rendered through warm atmospheric diffusion and organic surface stacking, avoiding sharp synthetic drops.

1. **Base Surface**: Warm linen tint (`#FDFBF7`) establishing the peaceful, low-strain baseline.
2. **Surface Containers (Level 1)**: Tinted oat tone (`#F7F3EC`) combined with a faint border tint (`rgba(118, 106, 101, 0.08)`) and ambient warmth: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(118, 106, 101, 0.04)`.
3. **Interactive & Floating (Level 2)**: Crisp card surfaces with elevated warmth: `0 10px 30px -4px rgba(217, 101, 59, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(118, 106, 101, 0.03)`.
4. **Overlays & Dialogs (Level 3)**: Frosted warm glass backdrop (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with warm stone drop: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(78, 112, 88, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(118, 106, 101, 0.04)`.

## Shapes
Shapes echo worn pebbles, soft river stones, and organic curves. Form elements adopt a unified `roundedness: 2` (base 8px / 0.5rem radius; containers at 16px / 1rem; large prompt boards and cards at 24px / 1.5rem). Interactive pills and tag markers use maximum curvature (`rounded-full`) to offer an inviting, approachable tactile quality. Sharp 90-degree corners are avoided across all interface elements.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Terracotta (`#D9653B`) with white text, using `rounded-xl` (16px) or full pill geometry. Hover transitions smoothly into a richer sunset tone (`#C2532B`) with a soft matching ambient glow.
- **Secondary**: Soft Sage tint background (`rgba(78, 112, 88, 0.10)`) with deep Sage text (`#4E7058`). No hard border. Transitions to `rgba(78, 112, 88, 0.18)` on hover.
- **Ghost/Tertiary**: Twilight Lavender text (`#6D5B8A`) with clear background; subtle lilac wash (`rgba(109, 91, 138, 0.08)`) on interaction.

### Chips & Badges
- Fully rounded pills (`rounded-full`) with light tonal backdrops matched to their intent:
  - *Calm/Grounding*: Pale Moss (`rgba(78, 112, 88, 0.12)`) + Sage text (`#4E7058`).
  - *Reflection/Evening*: Pale Lilac (`rgba(109, 91, 138, 0.12)`) + Lavender text (`#6D5B8A`).
  - *Energy/Vitality*: Pale Peach (`rgba(217, 101, 59, 0.12)`) + Terracotta text (`#D9653B`).

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Text Inputs**: Inset surface tint (`#F7F3EC`), minimal 1px border in stone tone (`rgba(118, 106, 101, 0.16)`), rounded to 12px. Focus rings present an unhurried, warm terracotta glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(217, 101, 59, 0.20)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Smooth rounded-md (checkbox) and circular (radio) forms. Unchecked borders use warm stone neutral; checked fills with Sage (`#4E7058`) featuring a white checkmark or dot.

### Cards & Reflection Panels
- Warm oat or alabaster fills (`#FFFFFF` on `#FDFBF7` base), `rounded-2xl` (24px), framed by an ultra-soft border (`rgba(118, 106, 101, 0.08)`). Content blocks employ generous interior padding (24px to 32px) to keep thoughts and exercises unhurried.

### Additional Specialty Components
- **Breathing Metronome**: Concentric organic rings using animated scaling with Sage and Twilight tints.
- **Mood Dial**: Tactile, curved slider filled with a soft multi-stop gradient shifting from Moss (`#4E7058`) to Lavender (`#6D5B8A`) to Terracotta (`#D9653B`).
- **Journal Prompt Accordion**: Frameless expandable lists divided by hairline stone rules (`rgba(118, 106, 101, 0.12)`) with soothing ease-in-out height transitions.
